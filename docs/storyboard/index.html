Mouth rule (from the lip-sync diagnosis)

Seedance re-performs a reference line: it adds 0.4–1.3 s lead-in and stretches the line 5–20%. Lips only hold when the speaker faces camera in a medium or close shot, and reference audio can't be combined with start/end frames. So:

generate as lip-sync shot one speaker, facing camera, mouth visible for the whole line, duration = line + 1 s.
mouth hidden back, over-the-shoulder, phone or offscreen: the line is laid in post, and start/end frames can be used for continuity.
clip carries its line older clips already lip-synced to their own line; they now play their own audio.

Blocking diagrams

Every set is drawn top-down from the game's own geometry (index.html / world.js), north up, ocean (east) to the right, like the town map. A set looks the same every time it comes back.

What this board fixes

  • Opening lines vs mouths. Every line is now either its own single-speaker lip-sync shot facing camera (1.3, 1.4, 1.6, 1.7, optional 1.8) or played over a back or offscreen (1.4A, 1.9). The two-speaker clip ch1_v2_5 is split.
  • Fly-in clips through buildings and shows the shop twice. A new path from the ocean, staying above the 19 m roofline until it is over the open square, lands on the exact engine frame. The shop appears once.
  • Shot-to-shot jumps. Each scene has one 180° rule drawn on its plan. Four clips are flopped for free (1.11, 2.1, 3.1, 3.3) and the rest are trimmed.
  • Missions don't make sense. A story spine with want → obstacle → why here → payoff per chapter, new goal calls that say why, and three new payoff shots (3.5, 3.7, 4.4).
  • Vaping the bosses like villains. The diva and Prime become a duet and a contest. Detecto stays the antagonist, because detecting smoke is literally his job.

Story spine: why, what, payoff

One week, one absurd problem. Each chapter: what Sal wants → what's in the way → why the bro goes HERE → how the out-scene pays it off. The loop every chapter: hook 5 in the district → they follow in a conga line → walk them to Cloud Kingz → Sal counts them in live.

Ch 1 The Typo

Monday · Downtown

Want
Sal fat-fingered an order: 5,000 vapes instead of 50. The invoice is due Friday or the bank takes Cloud Kingz.
Obstacle
Coral Vista won "Cleanest Air in Florida" in 1994 and still has the trophy. Nobody here has ever seen a vape.
Why here
Downtown is right outside the shop door. Start with whoever walks past.
Payoff
The five come back the next morning on their own, and they bring friends. Sal: "They're coming BACK!" Word of mouth is born.

Ch 2 Word of Mouth

Tuesday · The Park

Want
Go viral the Coral Vista way: through the grandma phone tree. One grandma tells forty.
Obstacle
Every grandma in town is pinned to a park bench, because nobody walks out on La Diva in the middle of an aria. She is rehearsing her comeback for Friday's Breathe Easy Day.
Why here
The park is where the phone tree lives, and the diva is the cork in the bottle.
Payoff
One puff turns her high C into a perfect cloud ring. She ends the aria in triumph, sings the grandmas into your conga line and buys every mango in the shop. Sal: "I sold out of mango!"

The diva (and the "why am I vaping a villain" problem)

She shouldn't be a villain. Nobody in this story is evil: they're obstacles with wants. Three roles, then three claymation looks to pick from. Nothing gets built until you choose.

A · The captive audience recommended

La Diva Lungarotti, Coral Vista's only celebrity (she sang at the 1994 Cleanest Air ceremony), is rehearsing her comeback aria for Friday's Breathe Easy Day. Nobody walks out on an aria, so every grandma in the park is pinned to a bench. You can't recruit anyone while she sings.

Interaction: offer her ONE puff, timed to the top of her phrase (the existing rhythm ring). Her high C comes out as a chain of perfect cloud rings. She's thrilled, ends the aria ("BRAVISSIMA!"), and the grandmas are free and follow you. From then on she's the shop's hype-woman: her next aria pulls nearby grandmas toward your conga.

Payoff: she buys every mango in the shop, so Sal's "I sold out of mango!" lands. Works with the existing 2.2 and 2.5 clips (ears covered = too polite to leave).

B · The rival act

She thinks the bro is a street performer stealing her audience. It becomes a sing-off vs cloud-off: each perfect rip wins one audience member, and each of her notes blows you back a step (keeps the current shove mechanic, now as showbiz rather than violence).

Payoff: she concedes the crowd with a deep bow and becomes your fan. Keeps the most of today's code but is the least original.

C · The lost voice

She has lost her voice two days before her comeback; the doctor prescribed "steam." The bro offers his "humidifier." One puff and her voice comes back two octaves higher, and the grandmas weep and follow.

Payoff: sweet and simple, but the lightest gameplay of the three (one walk-up, no skill).

Gym Bro Prime: same problem, flagged

Today he's a 3-HP boss you vape into submission. Fix: he's the beach's gatekeeper ("nobody here breathes till Prime breathes first") and challenges you to a three-rep lung set while his bros ring you. Same input (perfect rips, his exhale shoves you back), but framed as a contest he respects. Payoff: he hands you his gold chain in tribute (3.5), the bro hands it to Sal, and Sal's "Two chains, kid. TWO." is literal.

Detecto: keep him as the antagonist, fix the verb

He's the one character whose opposition makes sense: detecting smoke is his job, and the haze woke him up. Fix the verb, not the role: his alarm pulse clears the square of customers. You don't hit him; you fill his sensor three times until he gives a happy low-battery chirp and falls asleep standing up. Then the last 250 sell.

Fly-in path (opening → first gameplay frame)

One continuous glide, east to west along the game's x = 0 line. Ocean, beach, Muscle Beach and two downtown blocks, then it drops into the open square over the container and settles on the street facing Cloud Kingz. Blocks along the line can be up to 19 m tall, so the camera holds ≥ 22 m until it has cleared the last block edge (z −15.5). Only then does it descend, over open plaza. The shop never enters frame before the last two seconds, and the camera never goes past z 8, so it can't touch the shop.

Map: coast on the right (east). Dashed line = camera path, wedge = landing view toward the shop.
