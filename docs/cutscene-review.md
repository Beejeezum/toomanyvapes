# Cutscene review (shot by shot)

Reviewed from frame strips of all 16 original clips (start / ⅓ / ⅔ / end of each).

## What was wrong overall
- **Talking heads, no action.** The lip-sync clips came out almost static: one locked medium shot, a mouth moving, little body acting. Props drift (the floating invoice in Sal's first shot).
- **One shot per scene.** No wide → close-up → reaction cutting, so lines land on nobody.
- **Order and motivation.** Ch1 had Sal panicking before the bro even arrived; the bro's line came after. Nothing showed who Sal was yelling at.
- **Dry audio.** Only the voice line played: no footsteps, door bells, phone slams, room tone. That is most of why delivery felt flat.
- **Every shot did the same slow push-in**, so cuts felt repetitive.

## What changed in the ch1 reshoot (done)
1. Wide: the bro walks in past the pigeon, it flaps off, he stops at the container. (foley: feet, pigeon, gulls)
2. Close-up: "Duuude… that's like… a lot of vapes."
3. Through the shop door: Sal yelling at his supplier ("Fifty! I said FIFTY!… Hello? HELLO?!"), slams the phone.
4. Over Sal's shoulder: he turns, sees the bro in the doorway: "Kid! Oh, thank God… sell these by Friday or I'm closing."
5. Two-shot: Sal slaps the vape into his hand, "Get this town HOOKED!" / "Say less, boss. I got you."
Clips now carry their own voice + foley; hard cuts inside the scene; straight into Sal's goal call.
